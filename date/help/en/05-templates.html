Templates

A template is an export file that comes with the package. You use it to start from a known state.

The two templates

  • bloodlines.json has the default tree, with no keys.
  • empty.json has an empty tree. It keeps the template "teleport", so a macro that you add can use it.

The files are in /usr/share/bisonus-macro/templates/. Templates that you put in ~/.local/share/bisonus-macro/templates/ show first in the list.

Start from a template

  1. Open the main window.
  2. Click "New from template...".
  3. Choose a template.

The program saves your present state as a backup, then replaces the tree. This is the same as "Replace all". A template has no general settings, so your keys, pauses and theme stay. Your settings for the nodes go away with the old tree.

Use your export as a template

An export of yours works as a template. Click "Import..." and choose the file. Then click "Replace all". To make your file show in "New from template...", copy it to ~/.local/share/bisonus-macro/templates/.

See "Import and export" for the backup and the two kinds of import.
